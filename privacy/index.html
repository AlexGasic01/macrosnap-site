Privacy Policy

MacroSnap — AI Calorie Counter

Last updated: 5 March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MacroSnap ("the App"), an AI-powered calorie and nutrition tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the App. By using MacroSnap, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

MacroSnap is operated by Alex Gasic, based in Australia. If you have any questions, contact us at alex.digital200@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information when you use MacroSnap:

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address from those providers.
  • Health and nutrition data: Height, weight, goal weight, age, gender, dietary preferences, and daily calorie and macro targets you provide during onboarding.
  • Food log data: Meals, photos of food, and nutrition information you log within the App.
  • Usage data: Basic information about how you interact with the App, such as features used and screens visited.
  • Device information: Device type and operating system, used for technical support and app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and personalise the MacroSnap experience, including calculating your nutrition targets and tracking your progress.
  • Sync your data across devices when you are signed in.
  • Improve the App and fix technical issues.
  • Respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard security measures. We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Services

MacroSnap uses the following third-party services:

  • Anthropic Claude API: AI-powered food recognition and nutrition analysis — anthropic.com/privacy
  • Apple Sign In: Authentication via Apple — apple.com/legal/privacy
  • RevenueCat: In-app subscription management — revenuecat.com/privacy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

6. Camera and Photo Access

MacroSnap requests access to your device camera and photo library solely to allow you to photograph food for nutrition analysis. Photos are sent to the Anthropic Claude API to identify food items and estimate nutritional content. We do not store your photos on our servers beyond what is necessary to provide this feature.

7. Children's Privacy

MacroSnap is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at alex.digital200@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent to data processing at any time by deleting the App.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at alex.digital200@gmail.com.

9. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. To request full deletion of your data, contact us at alex.digital200@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
